## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do you need a CLA?

The CLA allows the project maintainer to use contributions in proprietary/commercial projects while keeping the public project under EUPL-1.2. This enables sustainable development and commercial support options without restricting the open-source nature of the public project.

### Do I lose rights to my contributions?

**No.** You retain full copyright to your contributions. The CLA grants the maintainer additional licenses to use your work, but you can still use your contributions however you wish (including in your own proprietary projects).

### Can I still use my contributions elsewhere?

**Yes, absolutely.** You retain all rights to your contributions and can use them in any way you choose, including in your own proprietary or open-source projects.

### What does "proprietary projects" mean?

It means the project maintainer can incorporate your contributions into closed-source, commercial software products without being bound by the EUPL-1.2 copyleft requirements for those specific uses.

### Is this common practice?

Yes. Many open-source projects use similar CLAs to enable commercial licensing while keeping the public project open-source. Examples include MongoDB, Qt, MySQL, and many others.

### What if my employer owns my work?

If your employer has rights to intellectual property you create, you need your employer's permission before contributing. Your employer may need to sign a [Corporate CLA (CLA-CORPORATE.md)](CLA-CORPORATE.md). If you're unsure, please consult your employer before contributing.

### Can the license terms change later?

The public project remains under EUPL-1.2. The CLA grants the maintainer rights to dual-license or use contributions in proprietary projects, but this doesn't change the public license. Your contributions will always be available under EUPL-1.2 to the public.

### What if I don't agree with the CLA?

That's completely fine! You're under no obligation to contribute. If you don't agree with the CLA terms, please don't submit contributions to this project.

### Is there any compensation?

Contributions are voluntary and unpaid unless a separate agreement is made. You're contributing to an open-source project that benefits the community while granting the maintainer additional commercial rights.

### Can I withdraw my contribution later?

Once accepted, contributions cannot be withdrawn, as others may have relied on them. However, you retain your copyright and can continue to use your work as you see fit.

### What law governs this agreement?

The CLA itself follows general principles of copyright and contract law. The public license (EUPL-1.2) is governed by EU law as specified in the EUPL-1.2 license text.
